About Brain TDS Logistics

Built on trust, speed and dependable service.

At Brain TDS Logistics, we are committed to providing reliable, affordable, and efficient logistics and delivery solutions across Ghana and beyond. Based in Koforidua and in Accra, we specialize in cargo shipping, courier services, last-mile delivery, and international freight forwarding, helping individuals and businesses move goods with confidence.

With our extensive network and strategic presence across the Eastern Region and Ghana as a whole, we ensure that parcels, documents, commercial goods, and personal shipments are delivered safely and on time. We also facilitate air and sea shipments from China and the United Arab Emirates (UAE) to Ghana, offering competitive rates, transparent pricing, and dependable transit times.

Our mission is to simplify logistics by delivering exceptional service, innovative solutions, and unmatched customer support. Whether you are importing goods, running an online business, or sending packages to loved ones, Brain TDS Logistics is your trusted partner every step of the way.
